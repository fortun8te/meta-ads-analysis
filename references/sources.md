# Primary sources and limits

This workflow is an original synthesis of measurement principles and practical analysis safeguards. Sources below support their stated topics; they do not certify this code, predict an account's winners or prove improved advertising results. Check current primary documentation when mutable platform behavior affects a decision. No external skill, paid course or client dataset is distributed here.

| Topic | Primary source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Insights and reporting | [Meta Insights](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/insights/), [pagination](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/results/), [breakdowns](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/insights/breakdowns/) | Verify fields, effective parameters, continuation and reporting limits against the current source. |
| Metric identity | [Ads Action Stats](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/ads-action-stats/) | Distinguish event aliases, counting and attribution variants. |
| Creative coverage | [Asset Feed Spec](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/ad-creative-asset-feed-spec/) | Multiple assets and variants require explicit inspection coverage. |
| Commerce attribution | [Shopify marketing reports](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/marketing-reports), [customer reports](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/customers-reports) | Preserve report/model, customer and revenue definitions when bridging platforms. |
| Observational versus causal effects | [Gordon et al. (2019)](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mksc.2018.1135), [Gordon, Moakler and Zettelmeyer (2022)](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mksc.2022.1413) | Attributed outcomes and observational adjustment do not establish incremental advertising effects. |
| Experiment quality | [Microsoft sample-ratio mismatch](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/diagnosing-sample-ratio-mismatch-in-a-b-testing/), [pre-experiment planning](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/experimentation-platform-exp/articles/patterns-of-trustworthy-experimentation-pre-experiment-stage/) | Validate assignment/logging and plan a useful effect, duration and stopping method. |
| Sequential monitoring | [Johari, Pekelis and Walsh](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.04922) | Repeated monitoring needs a justified sequential method for formal claims. |
| Proportion intervals | [NIST](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm) | Use interval methods only when the independent binary-outcome model is justified. |
| Agent skills | [OpenAI skills](https://developers.openai.com/codex/skills), [skill evaluations](https://developers.openai.com/blog/eval-skills) | Progressive disclosure, scoped instructions and behavioral evaluation separate from unit tests. |

Weighted ratios, overlap detection, requested-scope validation, source hashes and native-total reconciliation are local analytical safeguards. Matching totals support consistency under supplied coverage assertions; they do not independently prove complete extraction or causality.
